# Superagent 1.9.3-beta.12

- **A tidier list of chats.** Every chat under a project is the same height and starts on the same edge. The second line says which branch it is on and when it was last used; a chat on no branch just says when. The unread dot and the working spinner moved to the end of the name, the × no longer holds a gap open on the right, and there is a little room between one chat and the next.
- **Bars in the usage list.** The list at the foot of the sidebar shows each limit as a bar beside its figure, amber from 75% and red from 90%, and the account the chat is running on is filled in rather than only ticked.
- **A recoloured Dock icon is the right size.** A custom colour or picture came out a size larger than the icons beside it, and so did going back to the original.
