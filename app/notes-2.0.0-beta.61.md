# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.61

**The simulator pane does what Apple's Simulator does.**

- **Rotation works again.** Xcode 27 ignored the old way of turning the device; it now gets the same message Apple's Simulator sends. The rotate button, ⌘← and ⌘→, and agents' rotation all turn it.
- **Pinch.** Pinch on the trackpad over the simulator, or hold ⌥ and drag (a second finger mirrors yours, as in Apple's Simulator).
- **Keyboard shortcuts.** ⌘⇧H for Home and ⌘L to lock. Shift, Option and Control with arrows and other keys reach the device, so selecting text and jumping by word work. Other ⌘ shortcuts stay Superagent's.
- **A Device menu (⋯).** Light or dark appearance, text size, location, Face ID or Touch ID, shake, save a screenshot, and record the screen.
