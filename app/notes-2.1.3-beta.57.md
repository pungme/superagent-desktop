# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.57

**Update without waiting.** "Restart now" no longer means losing the work in flight: the chats that were mid-turn are written down, and when the new version is up each one is told to carry on. You don't have to wait for the agents to finish, or type "continue" yourself.

**Conductor on the phone.** Worker sessions show with their number (#101 …) in the phone's chat lists, and closed sessions are hidden, as on the Mac. The Conductor's Main chat is a normal chat on the phone, so you can talk to it from there.

**Tested.** Unit tests for the restart note and the carry-on message, for the session marks sent to the phone, and on the phone side for the new fields. Not yet: a real update installed with agents running.
