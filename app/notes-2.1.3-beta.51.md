# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.51

**Conductor: a worker's question reaches you in Main.** A session waiting to be allowed to do something now shows in the Main chat itself, above the composer: what it wants, with **Approve** and **Deny**. Nothing shows while no session waits, and nothing shows in any other chat.

Tested against a real agent: in Auto, writing a clear task to Main starts a numbered, titled session in about fifteen seconds.
