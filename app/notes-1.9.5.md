# Superagent 1.9.5

- **A loop keeps going.** An agent could end its own loop as soon as it had finished what it planned, which for "keep improving this" was after a round or two. It is now told that finishing its plan is the cue to look for the next thing: what is still weak, untested, unverified or unpolished. It ends a loop only when the thing it was waiting for has happened, when it cannot move without you, or when it has looked for more to do more than once and found nothing. When it does stop, it says what it checked.
