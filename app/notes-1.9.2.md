# Superagent 1.9.2

**More than one account, and how much is left**

- Add a second Claude or Codex subscription in Settings → Agents. When one runs out, the chat offers to carry on with another, or switches by itself if you choose Automatic.
- Every account shows its 5-hour and weekly usage and when each resets: in Settings, at the foot of the sidebar (hover it to see them all and switch), and on an Account pill under the composer.
- Out of one model? The chat carries on with the next one down and goes back once its allowance resets.
- Antigravity joins Claude Code and Codex as a third agent, chosen per chat.

**Your own browser**

- A project's agent can browse in your real Brave, Chrome or Edge, with its own Superagent profile. Bring the sign-ins of the sites you pick over from your everyday browser, and the agent sees the tabs you open there.
- When it hits a captcha, a login or a two-factor code, it stops and asks you, and carries on when you press Done. Google sign-in works too.
- Each chat keeps its own browser.

**Projects made of several repos**

- A chat in a folder of repos works in its own copy, and only copies the repo it is about to change. Keep and Throw away work across the repos it touched.

**Around the app**

- Settings is redesigned: one card per agent with its accounts underneath, settings grouped into cards, and a section list with matching icons.
- Chats is a section in the sidebar, above Projects. Pin chats, mark them unread, close them from the sidebar, and switch sessions with ↑ and ↓.
- Connect Apple Mail and the agent can search and read your mail and save drafts. It never sends.
- Attach the screenshot you just took from above the message box, in whichever chat you're looking at.
- Loops can be paused and resumed, from the Mac or the phone.
- The agent says which message it is answering when it isn't your last one.
- More projects show their own icon, including Swift packages, Electron and Tauri apps.
- A short intro film plays once, and Settings → About can show it again.

**Fixes**

- Claude no longer shows as signed out once its weekly allowance is used up.
- The message box no longer shrinks to a sliver.
- Clicking a chat while Settings is open opens it.
- A chat waiting on a background agent keeps running when you leave it.
