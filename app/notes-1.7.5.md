## Superagent 1.7.5

- **No keychain prompt on launch.** The phone companion asked macOS for keychain access the first time the app started, whether or not you use a phone. Now it asks only when you start pairing — and Settings → Phone tells you it's coming and to choose Always Allow.
- **Chats.** A Chats tab next to Computer: click it and you get just the chat app — conversations on the left, the current one beside them — no desktop around it. Simple mode.
