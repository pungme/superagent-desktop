# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.14

**If the app has been crashing, there is now a way to tell us why.** In Settings → Advanced, next to the logs, **Copy diagnostics** puts one block of text on your clipboard: the version and your Mac, when the app started (a restart after a crash shows as a start), the crashes and hangs it noted, and the macOS crash reports that name Superagent from the last week, cut down to what says why. Paste it into a message. None of your conversations is in it.

**The app now writes down its own crashes.** A window that goes white, a helper process that dies, a window that stops answering, and an error nobody caught each leave a line in the log, where before there was nothing to read.
