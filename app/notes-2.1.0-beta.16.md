# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.16

**A routine shows under the conversation that set it up.** A monitor or check an agent schedules now sits under that chat in the sidebar, not loose at the bottom of the project where nothing said where it came from. Routines made before this update are matched to their chat when the app starts. A routine you made yourself, or one whose chat has been deleted, stays under the project.
