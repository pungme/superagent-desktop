# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.19

**Computer use**

- **It can wait for the right moment.** Instead of waiting a fixed number of seconds, an agent can wait until a named control appears ("Done", a result) or goes away ("Loading…"), for up to 30 seconds.
- **Stop means stop, also while it waits.** A wait ends the moment you stop it.
- **Your yes is checked where the looking is done**, not only where the agent asks: no route to the screen skips it.
