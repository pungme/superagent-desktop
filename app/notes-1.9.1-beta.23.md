# Superagent 1.9.1-beta.23

- A project that holds several repos folds them away from its own row: a caret after the name shows or hides them, and it remembers your choice. The separate "2 repos" row is gone.
- Quitting Superagent now also stops routine runs that were in progress, and everything they started. Chat agents were already stopped on quit.
- Sending a message scrolls the chat to the bottom so you see it, even if you'd scrolled up to read.
