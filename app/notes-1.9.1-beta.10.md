# Superagent 1.9.1-beta.10

- Permission and prompt-injection asks are no longer a window-wide modal that blocks every other chat. They now show right in the chat that's asking, above the composer — a chat you aren't looking at shows "Needs you" in the sidebar instead.
- The token count next to "Working…" now actually ticks up as the reply streams in, instead of sitting frozen — and it's a true running total for the turn, not just the last request's own count.
- Pinned rows: the folder/pin icon and its status dot now stack vertically instead of sitting side by side, so titles get a bit more room.
