# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.2

- **Use your real browser.** A new Browser pill under the message box lets a project's agent browse in your real Brave or Chrome instead of the built-in browser. It runs with its own Superagent profile: sign in once, and your logins, extensions and password manager work there. Sites that block built-in browsers, like Google sign-in and many Cloudflare checks, see a normal browser. You still watch it live next to the chat, with an Open window button.
- **When the agent is stuck, it asks you.** On a captcha, a login, a two-factor code or an "are you human" check, it stops and shows "The agent needs you" with what to do. Solve it, press Done, and it carries on. You see it in the chat, as Needs you in the sidebar, and on your phone.
- Permission cards' titles were invisible in dark mode (white on white). Fixed.
