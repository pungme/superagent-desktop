# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.13

- **Signing in no longer locks the agent out.** After "Sign in yourself" (or when Google refuses a sign-in), the agent gets its browser back by itself: when you close that window, when you send any message, when your Google sign-in is saved, or after ten minutes. There's also an "I'm signed in" button on the pane. You no longer have to quit Brave.
- **Chats is a section in the sidebar**, above Projects. Your chats are listed right there; click one to open it. The pencil in its header starts a new chat.
- The "The agent needs you" card is readable in dark mode.
