# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.6

**Your Mac's battery on your iPhone.** The Superagent iPhone app shows the MacBook's battery beside the Live pill (a bolt while it is on the charger, red under 20% on battery), so you can see from anywhere whether a Mac working with its lid closed is running low. It updates while the phone is connected; a Mac without a battery shows nothing. Needs the iPhone app's next update.
