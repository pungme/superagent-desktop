# Superagent 1.9.1-beta.2

- Pinned conversations now show under their own "Pinned" section instead of only sorting to the top — the Computer's Chats list and the sidebar's Activity tab.
- New `⌘K` command palette: search projects, chats, and commands, or jump straight to recent ones. Skills moved into the palette's Navigate section to free up the shortcut.
- Focus the composer with `⌘J`, stop the current chat's agent with `⌘.`.
