# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.34

**The page on your phone is the page as the Mac shows it.**

- **The phone's picture of a page has the pane's own proportions.** While the Mac was not showing a conversation's browser, it laid the page out as wide as the whole window to take the phone's picture, so the phone got a wide, short page that looked nothing like the column the pane is on the Mac. Now it is laid out at the size the pane last had on screen, and for a page the Mac has not shown yet, a column half the window wide and its full height.
