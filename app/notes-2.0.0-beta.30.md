# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.30

**A floor under working with the lid closed.**

- **Let it sleep when the battery is low.** With "Keep working with the lid closed" on, a closed Mac in a bag used to run its battery flat. Now, on battery, under a level you pick (20% unless you change it; or never), it is let go to sleep and you are told. Plugged in, or charged back past the level, it keeps working with the lid closed again. Settings → General, under the lid switch.
