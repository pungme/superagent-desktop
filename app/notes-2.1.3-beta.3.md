# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.3

**Browser first, tidier.**

- **The layout switch is two small icons** in the toolbar, like the buttons beside it: the chat in a column beside the page, or a small chat floating on the page. It used to be a heavy dark "Side by side / Browser first" pill.
- **The floating chat sits in the corner of the whole desk.** With the simulator open beside the page, it now floats over both, at the bottom right of the window, instead of in the corner of the page alone.
- **It always shows the conversation.** If the chat sent its messages before the floating chat had finished loading, the floating chat stayed empty until the next message; it now asks for them when it starts.
- **Shorter status lines.** "Terminal U=90A321FB… xcrun simctl boot" reads "Terminal xcrun simctl boot".
