# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.16

**Computer use acts on things by name.** Instead of aiming the pointer at pixels, an agent can now:

- **press** a button, checkbox or link by what it is called,
- **fill** a text field with text in one go,
- **pick a menu item** by its path, like "File > Export…",

all without moving your pointer. The list of controls it reads is numbered, and nothing is pressed if the window has changed since it was read.

Everything a click is held to applies here too: your yes, one conversation at a time, the apps that are out of bounds, the question the first time it works in an app, and the question every time before anything named Quit, Empty Trash, Send, Pay and the like.

**Password fields are recognised properly.** A password field calls itself an ordinary text field to the system; it is now known for what it is, never read and never filled.
