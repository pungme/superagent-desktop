# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.15

**The address bar finishes what you type.** Start typing a site you have been to and the bar fills in the rest, selected, so Enter goes there ("apps" becomes appstoreconnect.apple.com). When what you typed is inside the name instead ("conn" for App Store Connect), the site is the first suggestion and already chosen. Backspace takes the choice away, so Enter then searches for exactly what is typed.

**Dock icon.** Clicking the Dock icon a second time, when the first click did not bring the window in front of you, now pulls it onto the Space you are on and puts it on top. Each Dock click also notes the window's state in the log, so a click that still does nothing can be explained.
