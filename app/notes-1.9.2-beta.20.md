# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.20

- **A first launch opens on the Superagent reel.** A new install plays the reel's opening with its soundtrack while the app gets ready, then dissolves into it — instead of a logo and seven seconds of blank window. Once per install; Skip or Esc ends it.
- **Each chat keeps its own browser.** Switching one chat to Brave — or its agent choosing your browser — no longer switches every other chat in the project, including all your standalone Chats.
- **Standalone chats keep running when you go to another session.** Leaving Chats for a project used to stop them mid-turn.
- **The cursor stays in the message box after you send**, whether you press Enter or click Send.
- **Pinned chats' subtitles are easier to read.**
