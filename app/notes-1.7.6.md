## Superagent 1.7.6

- **Right-click in the browser now does something.** The embedded browser had no context menu at all — a right-click was silently swallowed. It now offers what actually applies: Copy Link on a link; Save Image As…, Copy Image, and Copy Image Address on an image; Cut/Copy/Paste/Select All in a text field; Copy and Search Google for a selection; Back/Forward/Reload/Copy Page URL on a plain page. Works on the side-by-side phone view too.
