# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.1

**Split view: up to four conversations side by side.**

- **Open one beside the other.** ⌘-click (or ⌥-click) a chat in the sidebar, drag it out of the sidebar onto the window, choose **Open in Split** from its right-click menu, or press ⌘↩ on it in ⌘K. While you drag, the window shows where it will land.
- **A split is a row in the sidebar,** as in Arc. Open any other chat and the split waits under **Splits**; click it to come back to exactly the same windows. × separates it. It survives a restart.
- **Every window is a whole chat** with its own agent, composer and panes. Each shows its number, its project's icon and the chat's name; the focused one wears a blue ring, and typing goes there.
- **Move between windows** with ⌘1–4 (or ⌃⇧1–4), ⌃Tab, or ⌘⌥ and an arrow. Hold ⌘ to see the numbers. ⌘⇧↩ makes a window full size and back; ⌘W takes it off screen (its chat keeps running).
- **Arrange them:** two or three side by side or stacked (the button in a window's header, or drop near the top or bottom of a window), four as a grid. Drag the gap between windows to resize, or a window's handle to move it. Nothing is ever replaced by a drag — four is the most.
- **The browser and simulator fit narrow windows.** In a window too narrow to put them beside the chat, they become a mini inside that window — the page shown as it looks on a desktop, scaled down. Drag it to a corner, resize it, shrink it to a chip, or ⤢ to see it big.
- **The header fits the window**, dropping the path and labels first and never the chat's name.
