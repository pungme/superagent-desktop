# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.1

**The dot.** Superagent now also lives as a small tile in the bottom-right corner of your screen, over every app.

- **Ask from anywhere.** Click the tile or press ⌥Space, type or dictate, and the answer comes back beside it. You don't have to open Superagent.
- **Pick where it goes.** The box says "In Computer" or "In" a project. Click the name, or press Tab, to choose another; type to filter. Your pinned and most recent projects come first.
- **It becomes a normal chat.** Every request is a new conversation in the project you chose. "Open in Superagent" takes you to it, and it gets its own branch if it changes code.
- **You can see what it's doing.** The tile spins while it works and shows the last few steps, with a Stop button. It turns amber when it needs your yes, such as before sending an email, and green when an answer is waiting that you haven't looked at.
- **Drag it anywhere.** It remembers where you left it.
- **Turn it off** in Settings → General → The dot.

This is the first beta of 2.0. The dot can do what the agent can already do: commands, files, the built-in browser, the simulator and Mail. Clicking around inside other apps is not part of it yet.
