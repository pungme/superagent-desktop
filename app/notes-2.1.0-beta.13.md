# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.13

**A huge message no longer takes the iPhone app down.** A conversation with megabyte-sized messages (a pasted log, a whole file) could not be opened on the phone: it was sent every character of them. The Mac now sends the phone the first 60,000 characters of any one message and says how much it left out. The window still shows all of it.
