# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.6

**Computer use stays out of where your secrets are.**

- **Password managers are out of bounds.** While 1Password, Bitwarden, LastPass, Dashlane, Enpass, KeePassXC, Apple's Passwords or Keychain Access is in front, an agent can neither act nor take a screenshot. It is told to ask you to do that part.
- **Nothing happens on a locked Mac.** If the screen is locked, every action is refused rather than aimed at the password field.
- The same goes for a macOS password prompt.
