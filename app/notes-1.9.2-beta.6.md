# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.6

- Clicking a project in the sidebar opens the project again, not the New chat you just started and haven't used yet.
- A project with no conversation of its own shows "No conversation here yet" with a New chat button, instead of a blank page.
- A key typed straight after ⌘K is no longer lost: the search box has focus as soon as it opens.
