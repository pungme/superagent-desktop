# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.4

**Opening a split no longer stops your other sessions.** Entering, leaving or switching a split rebuilt every conversation in the window, and a conversation's agent lives inside it — so every running session stopped. Each conversation now stays exactly where it is while the windows move around it; a test holds the conversation itself (not just its text) through entering, leaving and coming back.

- **A drag can't get stuck.** A drag that ended somewhere unexpected left its preview on screen with the pages frozen behind it. It now always ends: let go anywhere, press Escape, or switch apps.
- **A calmer focused window.** No blue frame: the focused window has a slightly stronger edge, and the others' headers step back.
- **One ⋯ instead of a row of buttons.** Images, Keep, Throw away and New chat sit in a ⋯ menu in the chat's corner instead of floating over the conversation.
