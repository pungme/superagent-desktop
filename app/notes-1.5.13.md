### New
- **Browser omnibar floats as its own bar.** The address bar is now a separate rounded pill above the page, which is its own clean rounded rectangle — no more faked square corners.
- **✂ Snip lives on the pane.** A floating tool dock beside the page and simulator (room for more tools later) — freeze, drag a region, and it drops into your message.

### Monitoring & sessions
- **A session running a background monitor no longer dies when you switch away** — its work keeps it alive.
- **A green dot marks a session with background work** in the sidebar, so you can see a monitor is running even from another session.

### Fixed
- Dragging a group shows a clear insertion line, not a "drop inside" highlight.
- The git ↓/↑ badge refreshes when you return to the app (no more stale "to pull").
- Clicking a project in the sidebar closes the Settings page.
