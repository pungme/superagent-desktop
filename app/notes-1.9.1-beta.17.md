# Superagent 1.9.1-beta.17

- Projects no longer need a group. A fresh install starts flat, with an "Add a project" button at the top level — make a group later, if you want one, and drag projects into it. Dragging one back out puts it at the top level again.
- A group can now be deleted even if it's your last one, or empty. Its projects move back to the top level; they are never deleted with it.
- Dark mode reads properly again: Settings' Done button was white text on a near-white background, and the quiet grey labels on small buttons were too faint against the surfaces they sit on.
- The phone shows ungrouped projects the same way, with no header over them.
