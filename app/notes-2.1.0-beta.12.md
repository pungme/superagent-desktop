# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.12

**The ring round the agent's logo shows your usage limit,** not the conversation's memory. It is how much of the account's allowance is used, in the window nearest its limit: grey, amber from 75%, red from 90%. Hover the logo for the percentage, when it resets, and the memory as a line below it. The same ring is on iPhone and iPad.
