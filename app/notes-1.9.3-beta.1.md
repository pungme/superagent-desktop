# Superagent 1.9.3-beta.1

- **The agent can send email.** Ask it to send something and it does, from Apple Mail. You approve each one first: a card in the chat shows who it goes to, the subject, the attachments and the start of the message.
- **Formatted email and attachments.** Drafts and sent mail can carry an HTML body (a designed signature, styled text, pictures) and attached files, plus cc and bcc.
