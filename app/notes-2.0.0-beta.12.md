# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.12

**More of Superagent by asking** (from the dot or any chat)

- "Show me the wepush board", "open the files", "open the browser", "open the simulator": a project's panes, going to the project first when you name one.
- "What's on the portal todo?" and "put a card on the wepush board to fix the header": any project's todo list, by its name.

**Computer use sees better, and is harder to steer wrong**

- **It can read the controls by name.** Every button, field, checkbox, menu and link of the window in front, with what it is called and where it is, instead of guessing from pixels. What a password field holds is never read.
- **A closer look.** It can enlarge part of the screen to read small text.
- **It can wait** for a page or an app to finish loading, and ask which apps are showing without taking a picture.
- **It asks before clicking anything named Log Out, Shut Down, Empty Trash, Erase, Quit, Sign Out, Send, Pay or Buy**, every time, the same as for the shortcuts that do those things.
