# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.17

- **More than one subscription.** Settings → Agents → Accounts shows which account each agent is signed in as, whether it needs signing in again, and takes a second Claude or Codex account. When one runs out, the chat offers to carry on with another — or does it by itself in Automatic mode.
- **Out of Fable? It carries on with Opus.** When one model's allowance is used up, the chat moves to the next one down, sends your message again and says so. It goes back once the allowance resets.
- **Default means Default.** A chat that was once on Fable no longer stays on Fable after you pick Default.
- **The browser stays usable while the agent waits for you.** A permission request or "the agent needs you" froze the browser; you can log in or solve the check while the card is up.
- **Attach the screenshot you just took.** A new screenshot shows above the composer to attach or dismiss; several stack into one.
- **Fold a project's conversations away** with the caret on its row. It stays folded.
- **A browser for chats driven from the phone.** The agent no longer says the browser isn't open when you start a chat on the phone.
- **Simulator:** "Open in Simulator" no longer hangs when macOS's System Events is stuck, and the pane picks its device back up after a reboot.
- **Model menu** is wider, so its descriptions fit.
