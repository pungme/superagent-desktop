# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.28

**Sign-in and Connect windows can be closed.** They opened as a sheet with no title bar, so once one was open there was no way out. They are now ordinary windows with a close button, and Esc closes them too.

**They show their address, and you can change it.** A bar at the top shows the page's address. Type another one and press Return to go there, or use the back arrow.

**No more "Connect X, A, B" card.** A chat could offer to connect connectors that do not exist when the agent read a file that happened to contain Claude Code's wording. The card now appears only for connectors your account really has and that really need connecting.
