# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.76

**Restart to update always restarts.** If a window could not answer, the restart used to leave Superagent running with its windows gone and its phone link cut — and no way to quit it but Force Quit. Now the restart ends the process within seconds whatever a window is doing, and a quit you choose to call off leaves the app exactly as it was: phone connected, agents running.

**Each conversation sees its own simulator** (from beta.75, which did not publish). With two conversations in one project, the pane in one could show — and its agent then build onto — the other's device. The pane now keeps its device per conversation and follows only its own conversation's agent.
