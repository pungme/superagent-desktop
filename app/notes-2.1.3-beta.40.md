# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.40

**Contacts is a real table now.** Name with initials, a coloured status, the next follow-up date and your first custom field line up under column headers. Filter by status with counts, search always on. Open a person and every detail is there to click and fill in: status, email, who, ask, follow-up and your own fields, with the history as a timeline.

**Unused simulators delete themselves.** A simulator that is shut down, held by no active conversation and unused for 3 days is deleted automatically (Apple's own devices, running ones and ones you press Keep on are never touched). Conversations idle for days no longer hold simulators. Ask an agent to "delete my unused simulators" and it asks you in a dialog.

**Contacts, custom fields and account order.** Add any field to your contacts ("budget", "country") and the agent can fill and read them. A project's own account is picked before another one.

**Agents.** Antigravity sub-agents show in the research pill with progress; a stuck step is flagged; a hit limit is reported as a reply; the spinner shows for turns an agent starts itself. Superagent now keeps Antigravity and Codex up to date too.
