# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.3

**Split view, fixed and cleaned up.**

- **It works from every row.** ⌘-click (or ⌥-click) and drag now work on Pinned chats, Activity, and the chats of a folder of repos — not only the project tree. On a busy sidebar the first version did nothing at all.
- **Up to eight sessions.** Five to eight sit in two rows. ⌘1–8 (or ⌃⇧1–8) jumps to a window.
- **Clear at a glance.** Each window's header is its number, the project's logo and the session's name, in bold; the tools appear when you point at it. The single-chat header leads with the session's name too, with the project's logo beside it.
- **Hold ⌘** and every window shows its key — ⌘1, ⌘2… — in the middle of its header.
- **Name a split.** Double-click its row under Splits and type; the name replaces the list of projects. The row now lines up with Pinned, and no longer keeps a focus ring after a click.
- **A compact composer.** In a split window, or any narrow chat, the model, mode and account settings fold into one quiet line.
- **The mini page's border stays whole.** The page inside a mini sits inside its rounded edge instead of painting over it.
