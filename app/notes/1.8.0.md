## Superagent 1.8.0 — see what it weighs, get your disk back

**Settings shows what Superagent keeps on this Mac.** Settings → Advanced → Storage: a total, a bar, and five measured categories. On a machine that has been agent-browsing for a while, the biggest by far is browsing data — sites the agent visited storing their own caches and service workers, the way any browser profile grows — not your conversations. And inside the conversations, nearly all of it is pasted screenshots, not words.

**About 3.6 GB of dead browsing data cleans itself up.** 1.7.25 merged every project's separate cookie jar into one and deliberately deleted nothing. The old jars — dozens of complete browser profiles nothing has opened since — are now swept on launch, one launch after the merge, when they are guaranteed not to be in use. Logins were carried over back then; nothing you can feel is lost.

**Clear caches, keep logins.** A button on the Storage row removes the rebuildable parts — HTTP cache, service workers, compiled-JS cache — and never touches cookies or site data. Sites just re-download on the next visit. The numbers re-measure afterwards, so what was freed is shown, not claimed.

The 1.8 line consolidates the last week: accent colours and custom icons on both apps, replies, per-conversation model and simulator, the self-pacing /loop, the crash-dialog fix, and the pile of focus and pane repairs. If you skipped some 1.7.2x releases, this one carries all of it.
