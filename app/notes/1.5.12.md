### New
- **Snip to attach.** A ✂ Snip button on the preview toolbar: freeze the page or simulator, drag a box over any part of it, and the crop drops straight into your message — cursor ready — so you can point Claude at exactly what's wrong.

### Fixed
- **The preview pane's ✕ Close is back on the pane**, always present and clickable, for web previews and PDFs alike.
- **A background session opening a page no longer yanks you to it** — you stay in the session you're working in; the page is there when you go look.
- The simulator no longer flashes open and vanishes.
- Worktree names are readable in the sidebar without widening it.
- Browser preview lines up with its frame as the layout settles.
- Fixed a leak where an abandoned snip could freeze every preview until restart, plus a few smaller memory leaks.
- "Default" model uses your account's model; terminal steps read "Terminal", not "Running".

### Calendar
- Day / Week / Month with a real time grid, .ics import, and a layout that adapts when the window is narrow.
