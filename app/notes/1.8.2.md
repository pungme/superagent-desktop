# Superagent 1.8.2

- Every chat shows in the sidebar. A second or third conversation in a project folder, a chat created from your phone, a project with no branches yet — all of them now have a row. Before, they existed (your phone listed them) but the Mac had nowhere to draw them, so "New chat" could look like it did nothing.
- Clicking a PDF or image in the Files pane opens that file — not the one the pane happened to still have loaded from last time.
- The todo list's stage menu shows all four stages again. "Done" was being painted over by the card below the menu, so cards couldn't be marked done by click.
- A simulator you open on the Mac now mirrors to your phone immediately. It used to appear only after the agent had used a simulator tool in that conversation.
- A crashed page reloads itself in about half a second instead of leaving the pane blank.
- Your phone can send files, up to ten photos at once, and pause or stop background work; conversations report how full the context is for the phone's gauge.
- Board cards no longer show a "working" chip.
