## Superagent 1.7.29 — the crash dialog stops, and your colour survives daylight

**"A JavaScript error occurred in the main process", over and over.** When a page's renderer crashed — an OAuth popup fight was enough — the pane's dead view stayed registered, and the position-keeper re-attached it every quarter second: one crashed page, an endless stream of crash dialogs. A dead pane is now cleaned up instead of re-attached, and an unexpected error in the app's core is logged rather than thrown in your face as a modal.

**A chosen accent no longer vanishes in light mode.** The colour applied in dark mode and was silently overridden in light — so blue set at night was "gone" by morning, and clicking blue again looked like it did nothing. It was never lost; it was out-cascaded. Both themes respect it now.

**Clicking the Dock icon no longer bounces you away.** While an agent worked, the app treated every activation as a focus theft and handed itself back to your previous app — including when you deliberately clicked its icon. A Dock click is you asking for the app by name, and it wins now.

**Codex errors say what went wrong.** "Codex hit an error partway through this turn" now carries Codex's actual reason — a disconnect, a limit — instead of discarding it.

**/loop paces itself, like the terminal.** Without an interval, the agent now decides when each round runs — sleeping between rounds when checking again immediately would be pointless — instead of hammering continuously.

**The model belongs to the conversation.** Switching the model in one session no longer switches it under every other open session. Each conversation keeps its choice; the phone respects it too.

**Snip either side of a side-by-side view.** With the desktop and phone views open together, the drag now crops whichever picture it starts on. The phone used to sit on top of the crosshair, live and unsnippable.

**Sub-agents show as running again.** Background agents spawned by your agent appear in "Running in background" — a rename had made them invisible for a release.

**Drag a card out of Todo into the chat.** Its text lands in the composer to edit before sending; drop two and they stack into one message.
