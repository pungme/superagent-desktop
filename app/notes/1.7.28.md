## Superagent 1.7.28 — colour, and two conversations on two simulators

**Choose the accent colour.** Settings → Appearance: seven colours including pink, or the monochrome default. It changes the colour on your messages, on whatever is selected, and everywhere else the accent already appeared — and nowhere it did not.

**Give the app your own icon.** The same seven colours, or a picture of your own, which goes behind the white square rather than replacing the icon entirely. It changes the Dock icon while the app runs; Finder keeps showing the real one, because that lives in the signed bundle.

**Two conversations can drive two simulators.** Whichever simulator was booted used to be the target for every conversation, and two booted simulators was an error for all of them — so a second conversation could not use a second device, and two conversations sharing one installed over each other unaware. Each conversation now drives the device it opened, booting one says whose it is if another conversation is already there, and the device list marks yours.

**Drag a card out of Todo into the chat.** Its text lands in the composer so you can add to it before sending, instead of going straight to the agent the way "work on this" does. Drop two and they stack into one message.

**"This session ended" stopped appearing over conversations that were working.** The exit that raised it could belong to a process that had already been replaced, so the banner went up over the live agent that took over. Only the conversation's current agent can declare it dead now.

**A conversation deleted on the Mac disappears from your phone.** The Mac was deleting the row and telling nothing — not the phone, not its own other windows.

**The chat box cannot be squeezed out of existence.** The column it lives in had no minimum size, so it could vanish and leave the page filling the window with nowhere to type.
