## Superagent 1.7.27 — one page at a time again

**Two pages drew on top of each other, and on top of the app.** 1.7.26 gave every project a conversation so the chat box could come back — and the browser pane is identified by the conversation it belongs to, so projects that gained one ended up with two panes: the old one and the new one, both still on screen. A pane the app stops using is now taken down. If 1.7.26 left your window unusable, this is the fix; nothing else changed.
