# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.20

**A first run that shows the app, not a film.** The intro film is gone. A new Mac now opens on a short splash and the welcome card, then a tour of what Superagent does, page by page: real recordings of chats working in the sidebar, the agent trying a page in the browser and on a phone width, chats gliding into split view, and a routine being added. Notifications, the phone, the dot and using your Mac are drawn, and move into place. The picture is much bigger, the words say how to use each feature, and the last page lists the keys worth knowing. Settings → About → How it works plays it again.

**Your phone shows what is left of your plan.** The iPhone and iPad sidebar now ends with the same usage bar as the Mac's: the current account's logo, a bar for what is left and its reset. Tap it for every account by provider. A Mac on this version tells the phone which account a new chat would use, so the bar shows even with no chat open.

**Split view on iPad: one row per composer.** In a split, each window's message box is a single row. The agent's logo opens a menu with the agent, account, model, mode and browser, as on the Mac.

**Use the Mac's browser from your phone.** The page a chat has open now answers to your touch on the iPhone and iPad: tap to click, drag to scroll, pinch to zoom, and type with the keyboard and a row of keys (esc, tab, arrows, return). Switch to Trackpad for a pointer you move like a laptop's, with two fingers to scroll and hover that works. It reaches only that chat's browser in Superagent, never the rest of your Mac, and only from your paired phone. Needs the phone app's update too.
