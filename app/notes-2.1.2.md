# Superagent 2.1.2

**Dictation shows your words as you speak.** Hold the mic (or ⌥Space) and the words appear in the message box while you talk, about a second behind you, in the chat and in the dot. When you let go, the whole recording is read once more and the final text replaces the guess. It still runs entirely on your Mac.

**Dictation no longer freezes the window.** The speech model now runs in the background, so the app stays responsive while it works.
