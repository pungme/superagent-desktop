# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.21

**One logo, one job.** In a narrow chat (a split window, a column beside a page) the agent's logo in the message box is now the only place its mark appears, and all it does is open and close the settings. The line that repeated the settings is gone, the agent is a row like the others ("Agent · Claude Code"), and memory and tokens share one quiet line at the foot.

**Usage says what is left, everywhere.** The ring round the logo, its hover card, the Account row and menus now say "75% left", as the sidebar's usage menu does, instead of mixing used and left. The ring empties as you spend your allowance and still turns amber, then red, near the end.
