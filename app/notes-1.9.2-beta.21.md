# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.21

- **Sign in to Google inside the built-in browser.** Google's sign-in pages no longer answer "This browser or app may not be secure". If Google still refuses, the pane offers "Continue in Brave" in one click.
- **Mark a session as unread.** Right-click any session for "Mark as unread"; the dot stays, across a restart, until you open it again.
- **Close a chat from the sidebar.** Hover a chat in the Chats section and click its ×. A conversation you've used asks first.
- **↑ and ↓ switch sessions** while the sidebar has the keyboard (click in it first).
- **No more sessions appearing by themselves.** A branch an agent made for itself is still listed, but clicking it asks before starting a conversation on it. Agents are told not to leave worktrees behind.
