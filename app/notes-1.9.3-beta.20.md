# Superagent 1.9.3-beta.20

- **Tooltips and menus open over the browser, not behind it.** The Memory tooltip, the menus under the message box, the @ menu and the usage list were drawn underneath the page whenever they were wider than the column they open from. The page now steps aside for them and comes back when they close.
- **The folded settings open and close smoothly.** In a narrow chat, the list under the message box unfolds a row at a time and folds away again, and its arrow turns rather than flips.
