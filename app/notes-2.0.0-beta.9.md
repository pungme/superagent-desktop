# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.9

**Talk to the dot.** Press ⌃⌥ V from any app and the dot opens listening; press it again and what you said is sent. Nothing to click, and it is transcribed on this Mac. Change the shortcut, or turn it off, in Settings → General → Talk to it.

**Computer use**

- An agent can only use the Mac as the conversation you said yes to. Its tool address now proves which conversation it is, so one agent cannot borrow another's permission.
- Checked with a real agent: with computer use on it has its tools, and "open the e2e project" typed into the dot switches Superagent to that project.
