# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.39

**Routines, all in one place.** A new Routines page (in the sidebar, with a count of failed ones) lists every routine of every project, failed ones first, with its runs. It shows what a run costs from its own runs ("about 15k per run · 2.5M a week") and, when you slow a routine down, what that saves. Change what a routine does or how often it runs right there, pause it, or run it now.

**Incognito chats.** File → New Incognito Chat (⌥⇧⌘N), or the ghost button in Chats. Superagent keeps nothing of it: it never shows on your phone, and it is deleted when you delete it or quit, together with the agent's own log of the session. Your agent's provider still receives what you write, and the chat says so.

**The model pill shows what is really running.** When your pick runs out of allowance and a lower model takes over, the pill says so ("Opus 5.5 · not Fable 5.2") instead of showing your pick. Moving a chat to another agent and back now brings your model pick back too.

**Turn off all simulators, on request.** An agent asked to shut down every simulator now asks you in a dialog (Cancel is the default) and does it if you say yes. Also: a long step now says what it is, and switching a chat back to an agent resumes that agent's own thread.
