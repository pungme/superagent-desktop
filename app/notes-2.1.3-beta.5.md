# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.5

**The floating chat stays over the page.**

- **It overlays, it doesn't push.** In Browser first the page keeps its place and size when the chat opens (no more shrinking into a box); the card and the whole-chat drawer float on top. Dragging the card's left edge still makes it wider or narrower.
- **New keys: ⌘J and ⌘⇧J.** Ask Superagent (the box) is ⌘J and the whole chat ⌘⇧J. ⌘\ was opening 1Password, which keeps that key for itself. The sidebar still toggles with ⌘\.
