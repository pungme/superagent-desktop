# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.58

**Conductor on the phone.** The Conductor's Main chat has a Sessions button: every numbered session with where it stands (Working, Needs you, Idle), what it is asking to run, its branch, unmerged commits and changed files, the Confirm / Auto switch, and a tap to open any session. It refreshes while it is open.

**The phone loses its Computer row** (the Mac's Today replaced it), the composer's settings stay on one line on an iPad, and the chat holds its place better when older messages load as you scroll up.

**Tested.** Companion tests pass with the new `conductor.get` / `conductor.routing` calls; the phone's Sessions sheet was looked at in the simulator against sample sessions. Not yet against a live Mac.
