# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.38

**A Dock click opens the app again, dot or no dot.**

- **The Dock icon brings the window back while the dot is showing.** macOS brings an app's window back on a Dock click only when the app has none showing, and the dot counted as one: with the window minimised, hidden or behind, the click did nothing. A Dock click now always brings Superagent's window forward.
