## Superagent 1.7.24 — your phone can reach a Mac you left at home

**The Mac went to sleep and your phone could not find it.** It only stayed awake while a phone was connected or an agent was working, so putting your phone down let it go: a few minutes later the Mac idle-slept, the connection died, and the phone said it could not reach it. Waking the Mac fixed it instantly, which is what made it look like a connection problem rather than a sleeping computer. Keeping the Mac awake for a paired phone is now the default, and the switch in Settings → Phone says what turning it off costs.

**"This session ended" stayed up over a conversation that was working.** The banner was only ever cleared by the Retry button next to it, so if the agent came back any other way — you sent another message, it restarted itself — the warning sat there above a live reply. It now goes away as soon as the agent is up again.

**Switching one conversation to Codex no longer moves the others.** A conversation that had not started yet had no agent of its own and fell back to whatever the app's default was, so changing the default dragged every idle conversation along with it. Each conversation now keeps the agent it was created with.
