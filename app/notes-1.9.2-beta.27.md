# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.27

- **Settings, redesigned.** Each section has a heading, settings are grouped into cards, and everything sits in one centred column instead of stretching across the window. The section list has a matching set of icons, and About shows the version and updates in one place.
- **Clicking a chat leaves Settings.** Picking a chat from the sidebar's Chats list, or the Computer, while Settings was open used to open it behind Settings, so nothing seemed to happen.
- **More projects get their own icon.** Swift packages, Electron apps and Tauri apps keep their icon as a loose `.icns` file rather than in an Xcode asset catalog; those are now found, as is a PNG named like an app icon.
