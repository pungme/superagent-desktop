# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.7

**The ⌘ numbers no longer get stuck.** Taking a screenshot (⌘⇧4) could leave every window's ⌘1–⌘8 badge on screen for good. They now go away the moment ⌘ is not held, whatever swallowed the key.

**Go round the windows of a split with ⌘⇧] and ⌘⇧[**, like tabs in Safari or Chrome. Holding ⌘ now also lists every key that moves between windows along the bottom.

**A tidier simulator in narrow windows.** Home, Lock, App Switcher, Rotate, Fold and Open in Simulator move into the simulator's ⋯ menu when its pane is narrow, leaving the device picker, ⋯ and close.
