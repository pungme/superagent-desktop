# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.25

**The agent asks before it moves to your own browser.** When a site needs your logins, the agent used to switch the chat to your Brave (or Chrome or Edge) by itself, opening it and taking your focus. Now a one-line card asks first, with the agent's reason: "Claude wants to use your Brave: this shop needs your own login." Stay here or Use Brave; nothing opens until you tap. If you say Stay here, it keeps to the built-in browser, asks you to sign in there when it must, and does not ask again for half an hour. No card when you asked for your browser yourself, or picked it in the Browser pill. You can answer from your iPhone too.

**Why some accounts show no 5-hour limit.** The app now notes in its log which usage limits Anthropic reports for each account (their names only), so an account without a 5-hour row can be explained.
