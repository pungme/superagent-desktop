# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.15

**Finding a chat by what was said in it** now reaches every conversation that mentions it, however long ago. Before, a few recent, talkative chats could hide an older one: on a real list, 18 chats mentioned a word and the search saw 8.

**Computer use**

- Codex has a computer use of its own, on by default. In a Superagent chat it is now turned off, so the Mac is only ever used through Superagent's own tools: where you are asked first, some apps are out of bounds, and ⌥Esc stops it.
