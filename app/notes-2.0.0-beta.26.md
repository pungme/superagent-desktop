# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.26

**A reply that arrives.**

- **Replies come in a word at a time.** Each new word comes out of a blur into focus, at an even pace however unevenly the agent sends it, with a small breathing dot where it has got to. A finished reply is plain text, as before. If your Mac is set to reduce motion, words simply appear.

And for computer use:

- **Its settings are sealed.** They are kept encrypted where only Superagent can read them, so nothing else on your Mac can switch computer use on or empty your keep-out list. Updating turns computer use off once, and forgets "always allow" answers: turn it on again in Settings. Your keep-out list and look-only apps are kept.
- **Pasting what you copied is asked about,** like reading your clipboard. Text the agent put there itself pastes freely.
- **A yes covers only what was asked.** If what is under a click changes while you are reading the question, nothing is clicked. And a yes given after you pressed ⌥Esc does nothing.
- **Showing only the apps you allowed** now holds for a window brought forward at the moment the picture is taken.
