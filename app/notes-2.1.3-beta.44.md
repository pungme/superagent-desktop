# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.44

**"Conversation not saved … Transcript changed in another window" no longer stops saving.** When the phone added to a conversation that was open on the Mac, the Mac's save was refused and stayed refused, and Retry save sent the same stale copy again. The Mac now puts its copy and the stored one together (the phone's new messages, plus the reply it was streaming) and saves that, by itself and when you press Retry save.

Also in this beta, from beta.43: a message from the phone no longer restarts the agent (and kills its background monitors) when the phone carries another agent's model; a browser you close stays closed; Contacts is a table; a start screen for new people.
