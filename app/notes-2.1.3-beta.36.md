# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.36

**Going back to your preferred account never interrupts work.** When an account you were moved off has allowance again, the chat used to switch right away, which stopped anything still running in the background. It now switches when you send your next message, and only when nothing is running in the background. Your message carries on in the same conversation on the better account. Picking an account yourself always wins.
