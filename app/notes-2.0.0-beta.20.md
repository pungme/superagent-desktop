# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.20

**A tour of how it works.** Setting up now shows five short animated scenes before you start: your chats on one rail, the agent seeing what it builds, how it gets your attention, the dot, and using your Mac. The dot's switch is on its own slide. See it again any time from Settings → About → How it works.

**Computer use**

- **A ring shows where it is about to act.** Before a click, a drag or a scroll, a ring closes in on the point with a word under it. It takes no clicks, and is not in the agent's own screenshots. Turn it off in Settings.
- **A record of what it did.** Every look, every action and every refusal, with when and in which conversation. What it typed is not kept. Settings → General → What it has done.
- **Standing answers per app.** Mark an app "always allowed", so it is not asked about in each conversation, or "look only", so an agent may read it but never click or type in it.
- **Opening an app is checked first.** An app that is out of bounds is not brought to the front at all, and any other is asked about before it opens.
- **Clipboard.** It can put text on the clipboard to paste, and read the clipboard only with your yes, every time.
- **Straight to a Settings pane.** Wi-Fi, Displays, Privacy and the rest, without clicking through.
