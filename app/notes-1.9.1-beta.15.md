# Superagent 1.9.1-beta.15

- Start a chat without making a project first: the compose button on the Chats row (or File > New Chat) opens a fresh conversation straight away. The phone has the same button in its sidebar.
- The phone stays fast with lots of sessions running. It used to keep listening to every chat you'd ever opened, and the Mac resent the whole chat list on every turn in every chat.
- Opening a chat on the phone lands on the latest message again, instead of stopping partway up.
- Pinned rows show a bigger project icon, with its working / unread dot as a badge on the corner.
- A long background command no longer breaks its popover into a one-letter-wide column, and its pill is named after what it runs (an ssh job by its host) rather than a stray fragment like `done"`.
- A message's time no longer overlaps the "steps" row under it.
