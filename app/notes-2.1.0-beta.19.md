# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.19

**Usage at a glance, per provider.** The usage indicator at the bottom of the sidebar now groups accounts under the Claude, Codex and Antigravity logos. Each account shows a bar for what is left, so an exhausted limit reads as empty, and says plainly when a weekly limit blocks it even though hourly allowance remains. Hover an account to see its 5-hour, weekly and Fable limits with their reset times; click to keep it open, and use "Use this account" to switch the chat.
