# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.9

- **Google sign-in in Brave, Chrome or Edge now handles itself.** When Google refuses to sign you in, the browser reopens without the agent on the sign-in page. Sign in, and Superagent notices by itself, closes that window and gives the browser back to the agent. No ⌘Q, nothing to close. The agent waits for you instead of failing over and over.
- **Research agents stay visible.** An agent working in the background keeps its pill until it reports back, also after the turn ends or you send another message. Your phone's Running strip shows them too.
- **Browser button in the toolbar.** Files, Todo and Browser sit together as compact icons; Browser opens the pane without waiting for the agent.
- The Browser menu no longer runs off the window's edge, and shows each browser's icon.
- Each pane says which browser it is: "In app", or a live dot and the browser's name.
- A message queued to send later, and the reply bar, no longer cover the chat's last lines.
- **Reset Superagent** in Settings → Advanced starts fresh: it removes every project, chat, board and routine. Your files, paired phones and browser logins stay.
- Deleting a new chat right after its first message no longer leaves its branch behind as an empty row.
- Long pasted text in a chat collapses to a preview you can expand.
