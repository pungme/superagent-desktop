# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.28

- **See your usage at a glance.** The foot of the sidebar shows how much of the open chat's account is used. Hover it to see every connected account with its 5-hour and weekly limits, and pick one to move the chat to it.
- **Pick the account per chat.** With more than one account for an agent, an Account pill under the composer shows which one the chat is on and how full it is, and lets you switch.
- **Usage for every account.** Claude accounts added with a token now show their limits too, not only your main login.
- **Settings → Agents, tidied.** Each agent is one card: whether it's connected, its version, and its accounts with their usage underneath. Notifications moved into General.
- **Fixed: "Not signed in" when you were.** Claude showed as signed out once its weekly allowance ran out.
- **Fixed: a squashed message box.** The box sometimes showed as a thin sliver that cut the placeholder in half.
- **"Out until" says which day** when a limit resets on another day.
- **New group** is now a button next to New tab, in the Projects header.
- **The phone app shows usage and lets you switch accounts too**, once it is updated.
