# Superagent 1.9.1-beta.16

- An @mention now reads as a pill — `@wepush-creator-manager` rather than its whole absolute path sitting in the middle of your sentence. The full path still goes to the agent when you send. Backspace after one removes the whole mention.
- Drag a conversation onto Chats to take it out of its project, and out of any group with it.
- Typing stays responsive with several sessions running: a chat you aren't looking at no longer redraws itself every frame while its agent streams.
