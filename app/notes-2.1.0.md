# Superagent 2.1.0

Everything from the 2.1 betas, in one release.

- **Split view.** Up to eight chats side by side, from any project. ⌘-click a chat or drag it in; resize, rearrange, or give one the whole window. A split is a row in the sidebar you can name, ⌘1–8 jumps between its windows, and ⌘⇧] and ⌘⇧[ go round them. Opening one never stops your other chats. Four on iPad.
- **Your claude.ai connectors work.** Instantly, Superhuman, Attio, Gmail and the rest show under each Claude account in Settings → Agents. Connect signs in in a window of Superagent's, and a chat that needs one offers to connect it right there.
- **More than one Claude account, signed in fully.** Add an account and sign in inside Superagent; choose which one new chats start on. When one runs out, the chat moves to the next (or asks first).
- **Usage you can read.** How much of each plan's 5-hour, weekly and Fable allowance is used, per account, in the sidebar and on your phone.
- **Control your Mac from your iPhone or iPad (beta, off by default).** Its screen, trackpad and keyboard on the phone, allowed at the Mac each time with a six-digit code, with a red banner while it runs and ⌥Esc to stop it.
- **Use the Mac's browser from your phone.**
- **Skills in Settings,** and add them from anywhere: a folder, a zip, a .skill file or a GitHub link.
- **Background tasks under control.** At most four at a time, duplicates fold into one, and finished ones no longer pile up.
- **The agent asks before it moves to your own browser.**
- **A first run that shows the app.** A short tour of drawings of the app at work, then the keys worth knowing. No film.
- **When the agent goes quiet, the chat says why,** and messages you send meanwhile say they are waiting.
- **Projects:** rename one, move a chat into or out of one, and routines show under the chat that set them up.
- **iPhone and iPad:** much smoother long conversations, videos from the photo picker, ⌘V to paste a picture on iPad, and a faster reconnect.
- **Reliability.** The simulator no longer freezes the app, an agent finishing no longer reloads every project, less energy while the window is hidden, and the app writes down its own crashes so a bad day can be explained.
