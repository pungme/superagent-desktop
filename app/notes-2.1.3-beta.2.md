# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.2

**Smoother floating chat in Browser first.**

- Opening the whole chat (⤢ or ⌘⇧\): the side panel now slides in cleanly. The box used to fade out behind it, leaving grey smudges at its edge, and the panel's first moments were cut off. The box now steps aside at once and the panel slides in over a view that is already tall enough.
- Putting the box away (⌄, ⌘\ or Esc): the frosted sheet behind it now goes with the box, instead of lingering as a pale shape after the box has shrunk.
