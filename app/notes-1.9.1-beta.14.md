# Superagent 1.9.1-beta.14

- Holding Send on the phone to send once the agent finishes now happens on the Mac, not the phone — close the app, lose signal, whatever, it still goes out.
- Hiding the Simulator's window while it mirrors no longer freezes the picture the agent taps against, which was landing taps on wherever the UI used to be.
- That hide (and the "Open in Simulator" show) now only ever touches your own device's window, not every simulator another conversation on this Mac might have open.
