# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.1

**Browser first.** The page you are working on can take the whole window, with the chat floating over it in the corner. Switch with **Side by side / Browser first** at the top of the window, or ⌘⇧B.

- At rest it is just Superagent's icon, with the agent's logo on it and ⌘\ under it. Point at it and "Ask Superagent" unrolls; click it, or press ⌘\, and the box opens out of it.
- Your conversation stacks above the box, newest at the bottom, older messages fading; scroll to read further back. The box stays open until you put it away with ⌄, ⌘\ or Esc.
- While the agent works, a glow breathes round the icon with one line of what it is doing. When it answers, the answer peeks out for a few seconds and leaves a blue dot.
- ⤢ or ⌘⇧\ shows the whole chat down the side of the page; **Put beside the page** goes back to side by side.
- If the agent asks you something only the full chat can answer (a permission, a choice), it says so, with a way to the chat.

**⌘K no longer freezes the app.** With many chats, opening the command palette or typing in it could stall every window for seconds: each row looked up its project's icon by reading the project folder. Icons are now looked up once, and the palette shows the best 50 matches.
