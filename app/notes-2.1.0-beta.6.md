# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.6

**A quieter message box in narrow windows.** In a window of a split, or a chat beside a page, the chat's settings are now just the agent's logo at the front of the message box — no line of their own under it. Press the logo to see and change the agent, model, mode and browser. A small dot on it says when memory is nearly full.

**The sidebar stays put while you drag.** Carrying a chat from Pinned or the list moves it up and down only; the sidebar no longer slides or scrolls sideways under it.
