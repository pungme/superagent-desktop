# Superagent 1.9.3-beta.17

- **An unfolded iPhone Duo no longer ends up on its side.** Pressing Rotate on a simulator that does not turn (an unfolded Duo stays landscape) used to tip the picture sideways and leave it there for up to eight seconds. The pane now asks the device which way it really is straight after, keeps the picture upright, and says the device did not turn.
- **Agents can look at another conversation's simulator.** A screenshot or a listing of a simulator that another conversation is using is allowed; only commands that would change it are stopped.
