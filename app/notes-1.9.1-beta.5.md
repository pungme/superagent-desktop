# Superagent 1.9.1-beta.5

- Pinned rows now have a pin icon, support double-click to rename, and refresh immediately after a rename instead of waiting up to 5 seconds.
- A pinned folder-root chat now shows as the project itself (name + folder icon), matching its row in the tree, instead of a generic "New chat" entry.
- The browser tool (`browser_click`) can now click by pixel coordinate as a fallback when a target isn't a standard link/button — for dashboards that style a plain div/span as a control with only a JS click handler.
