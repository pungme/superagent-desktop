# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.13

**Today.** A new home screen at the top of the sidebar, where Computer was: your day on one screen.

- **Calendar.** Today's events from Superagent's calendar, with a line for now.
- **To-do.** The open to-dos from every project's list in one place. Tick one off, add one to any project, or press **Hand to agent**: it moves to Doing and an agent starts on it in a chat of its own in that project.
- **Agents.** What is waiting on you (with Allow and Deny right there), what is working, and what finished while you were away. The Today row says when something needs you.
- **Ask.** The box at the bottom starts a chat with your question.

Routines and the Dashboard still open on the Computer's desk. Apple Calendar, Reminders and a Today tab on the iPhone are not in this beta.
