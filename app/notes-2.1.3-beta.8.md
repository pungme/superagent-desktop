# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.8

**Allow "use your Mac" from your phone.** When an agent asks to use your Mac (see the screen, move the pointer, click and type), the iPhone app now offers Approve as well as Deny. Approve asks for Face ID or your passcode on the phone first, and the Mac shows a notification that your phone allowed it; ⌥Esc stops it at any time. A phone's yes covers that one request only, never the rest of the turn. Needs the iPhone app's next update.

A phone that says it checked Face ID is taken at its word, so this protects against someone using your unlocked phone, not against a hostile paired phone. Remove a phone you do not trust in Settings → Phone.
