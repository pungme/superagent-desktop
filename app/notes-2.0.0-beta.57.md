# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.57

**The menu bar menu looks like it belongs on a Mac.**

- **Proper sections.** "Working now" and "Recent" are real menu headers instead of greyed-out lines.
- **Two lines per conversation.** Its title, and under it the project and when it was last used, or that it is working now.
- **Icons.** Each recent conversation shows its project's own icon, as the sidebar does; one that is working shows a green dot.
- **The phone link at a glance.** A coloured dot and "Connected to your phone", with how many phones are paired.
