# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.5

- A `/loop` now shows on your phone: what it repeats, how often, when the next round is, and a Stop button. You can also start or stop one from the phone by typing `/loop` there.
- A loop keeps running when you switch to other chats. It used to stop once its chat was out of view.
- Interrupting the agent now ends its loop, instead of leaving a "Looping" bar that never ran again.
