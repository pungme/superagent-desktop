# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.9

**Chats that belong to no project can join a split.** "Open in Split", dragging, and ⌘K now work for a loose chat the same as for a project's chat. Before, the split quietly dropped it. They show as "Chats" in the window's title.

**Rename a project.** Double-click its name in the sidebar, or choose Rename… from its menu. Only the name in Superagent changes; the folder keeps its name. On iPhone and iPad, hold a project and choose Rename.

**Move a chat into or out of a project.** Right-click a chat and choose Move to, then a project or Chats. On iPhone and iPad, hold the chat. A chat that has been used keeps its conversation: the next message hands the new project's agent the conversation so far. A chat working on its own branch stays with its project.

**Split windows can be dragged by their title,** not only the small grip.

**iPad: tap the conversation to dismiss the keyboard,** also when a hardware keyboard is connected. A draft you were typing stays.

**Update after your tasks is a small chip** at the top of the chat instead of a bar across it. Hover for the full sentence, click × to cancel.

**⌘K results can be dragged onto the window** to make a split.
