# Superagent 2.1.3-beta.10

**Clicking the Dock icon should always bring the app back.** Two ways it could do nothing are closed: the window can no longer be left on a display that is gone (it is moved back onto your main display when you click), and Superagent now knows its own window for certain, so the dot or the remote-control banner can no longer be mistaken for it.

If it still happens, note what the Dock icon does (bounce or nothing), whether the dot is on, and whether you changed monitors, and tell us.
