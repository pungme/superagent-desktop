# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.77

**Pin a message.** Hover a message and click the pin on its rail. A strip at the top of the conversation shows what is pinned; click it to jump to the message (with several pins it walks through them), × to unpin. Pins are saved with the conversation, so they are there after a restart and on your phone.

**The dot keeps a diary.** Every time the dot is turned on or off, and every time its window is created, Superagent writes who asked to its `memory.log` — so if it ever comes back uninvited, the log says why.
