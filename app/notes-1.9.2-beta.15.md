# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.15

- **The agent's steps in Brave no longer hang.** Brave stopped drawing when another window covered it, so a screenshot waited for ever ("Working 500s…"). It keeps drawing now, and any step Brave doesn't answer within 30 seconds fails so the agent can try again.
- **Brave shows in the pane straight away** when you pick it, instead of "Waiting for the agent to open a page".
- **Click a queued message to see all of it.** The "sends when done" chip opens to the whole message; click again to fold it.
