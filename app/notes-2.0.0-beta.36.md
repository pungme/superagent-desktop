# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.36

**The dot gets out of the way, and is off until you want it.**

- **Off by default.** Turn it on in Settings → General, or while setting up; the tour's slide about it has the switch too. Anyone who had it on keeps it; anyone who had turned it off stays off.
- **Hidden while Superagent's own window is in front.** The dot is for asking from anywhere else; with the app up you are already here. It comes back the moment you go to another app, or close or minimise the window. (It was on screen the whole time before, over Superagent's own window too.)
- **"Switch to wepush", said to the dot, switches the dot.** The next request goes to that project; Superagent's window stays wherever it is. Before, it brought the window up, which from the dot is the opposite of what you asked for. Asked from a conversation in the app, the same words still show the project in the window.
- **Left alone, it fades to an ember.** After a few seconds with nothing open and nothing running, the tile shrinks and fades; the pointer finds it where it was and it is whole again at once, as it is when a request runs or an answer arrives.
- **Closing the window no longer takes the dot with it.** With an update downloaded, closing the last window used to quit the app to install it, dot and all, and nothing brought it back; with a beta every hour that was nearly every time. With the dot on it now restarts into the update instead, and the dot is back in a few seconds.
