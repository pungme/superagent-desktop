# Superagent 2.1.0-beta.29

**A tidier Settings → Agents.** Each account's name is clearer, and the account new chats start on carries a "Default" pill. The usage bars line up across every account.

**Connectors without the wall of buttons.** Connected ones show as small chips with a green dot. The ones not connected on that account fold into a single "+7 not connected" line; click it to see them, each with Connect.

**Token accounts in one line.** What a token can't do (claude.ai connectors, Claude in Chrome) is said once, next to a "Sign in fully" button.
