# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.8

- **Bring sign-ins over.** When a project uses Brave, Chrome or Edge, "Bring sign-ins over…" in the Browser pill copies the logins of the sites you pick (say Shopify and Google) from your everyday browser. Nothing else comes over, so your email and bank stay out of the agent's reach.
- **Google sign-in works in the agent's browser.** Google refuses to sign in any browser that's being driven. When it does, Superagent now pauses the agent so you can sign in, then hands back. In Brave this reopens the window without the agent; quit it with ⌘Q when you're signed in. "Sign in yourself…" in the Browser pill does the same whenever you like.
- Switch the Browser pill in the middle of a conversation and the agent now uses the new browser, instead of insisting its tools only reach the built-in one.
- When Superagent quits its Brave, a sign-in you'd just made is no longer lost.
