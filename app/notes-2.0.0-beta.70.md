# Superagent 2.0.0-beta.70

**Touch on the simulator follows your hand.** A pane opened while its device was still booting asked once whether the device took live touch, heard "not yet", and stayed on the slow path — whole swipes sent in segments, taps sent on release — for the rest of the session. That is the lag. It now keeps asking until the device says yes, usually within seconds of the home screen, and from then on a finger down is a finger down. The live path acks a move in under a millisecond on an M-series Mac.

**The simulator pane says when it is streaming** (from beta.69): a small "Streaming" pill with the reason while the screen cannot be embedded; gone the moment it is.
