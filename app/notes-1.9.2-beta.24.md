# Superagent 1.9.2-beta.24

- **⌘K, then type.** Picking a project, a chat or New chat from the command palette now leaves the cursor in the text box. No extra click.
- **Brave shows up right away.** Picking Brave streams the tab at the front of its window at once, and a tab sitting behind another one no longer leaves the pane empty.
- **Choose the browser from your phone.** The iOS app gets a Browser pill once it is updated.
- **Long conversations open faster on the phone.** A phone opening a chat for the first time is sent its last 400 events instead of all of them.
