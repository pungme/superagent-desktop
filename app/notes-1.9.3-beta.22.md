# Superagent 1.9.3-beta.22

- **Place your own picture in the app icon.** Picking a picture now opens it with a live preview of the icon: drag the picture into place, zoom, then Use this icon. Nothing changes until you do. The swatch in Settings shows the icon you made, and clicking it lets you adjust it again.
- **Any colour.** A colour picker beside the swatches, for the app icon and for the accent. A light accent gets dark text on it.
- **Look at a screenshot before attaching it.** Clicking a suggested screenshot shows it large. Attach is the button beside it.
- **An agent can end its own loop.** When a loop has done its job, or another round cannot help, the agent stops it and says why, instead of being woken again and again to say there is nothing left.
- **Keep working with the lid closed.** A new switch in Settings → General → Power, off by default. macOS asks for your password once. While Superagent is open your Mac will not sleep with the lid shut, so it stays awake, and warm, in a bag; quitting Superagent or turning the switch off gives sleep back.
