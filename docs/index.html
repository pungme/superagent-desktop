Three things a terminal can't do

It remembers, it browses, and it keeps going.

One chat that stays

A single, persistent conversation per project. Close the app, reopen it, and your agent still has the full thread — context and all. No re-explaining.

A browser it can drive

A real Chromium pane right beside the chat. The agent navigates, clicks and types on live sites — using the sessions you're already signed into. You watch it work.

Routines on a timer

Turn any task into a routine — "every morning, check this and follow up." It re-runs against your browser and your logins while the app is open.

Your machine, your keys

Everything runs locally on your Mac against your own Claude subscription. No middleman server, no extra account, no per-seat SaaS.

Open source

The whole app is on GitHub. Read it, build it yourself, or fork it. Nothing about how it drives your browser is hidden from you.

You stay in control

Switch models and permission modes per chat. Full access, edits-only, or a read-only plan mode — you decide how far the agent can reach.

Watch it, don't wait on it

Send it off, then get on with your day.

  • Message it mid-task — it reads you and adjusts, like the terminal never could.
  • Reply to any message to give the agent exact context.
  • Open PDFs, images and code right in the app — no bouncing to other windows.
  • Push-to-talk dictation, transcribed on your Mac.

Give your agent somewhere to live.
