Open & localオープン・ローカル

Don’t trust us. Read us.

An agent with your logins should have nothing to hide. Every line of Superagent is public.

Learn more

Open source.

The Mac app, the iPhone app and the relay, MIT licensed.

No server of ours.

Chats, code and pages stay on your Mac.

No account.

No sign-up, no tracking, no second bill.

Signed and notarized by Apple: the app you download is the build that was published. Bugs and plans are discussed in public. Claude Code talks to Anthropic, Codex to OpenAI, Antigravity to Google, directly, on your own subscription. Browser sessions are stored on your Mac, like any browser’s. Only when you pair a phone does the Mac use a relay, and it sees nothing but encrypted data; you can run your own with one command.

Read the code for the Mac app, the iPhone app and the relay, or see all three in one place.

The small things小さな事

Sweat the small stuff.

Survives restartsPanes, files and chats come back as you left them, even after an update.
Never steals focusAgents finish quietly. The window comes forward only when you ask.
Talk mid-taskType while the agent works. It reads your message before it finishes.
Context gaugeEvery chat shows how much of the agent’s memory it has used.
Useful notificationsDone, or a question, with a summary of the reply.
Updates itselfSigned updates in the background. It waits for running chats before it restarts.
Models stay currentNew models in your Claude Code appear without an app update.
Quit means quitQuitting stops everything it started: agents, background tasks, routines.
Stop really stopsStopping a turn also stops the builds and servers it started.
Pins in your orderDrag pinned chats into any order. Your phone follows.
Every picture, keptAll the images in a chat, by day. Download one, or jump to its message.
Light when idleA window with nothing to do uses very little CPU.
Your names stayA chat you named keeps its name.
