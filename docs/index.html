VIII.iPhone and iPad電話

Your Mac’s agent, in your pocket.

Pair the iPhone or iPad app once with a QR code. Then follow every chat as it happens, start a new task, or approve a request, from anywhere.

Messages are encrypted end to end and pass through a relay that can’t read them. You get the same sidebar and pins as on your Mac, and you can switch between Macs.

IX.The dot点Beta

Ask from anywhere.

The dot is a small button in the corner of your screen. Press ⌥ Space in any app and ask: it looks at what’s on your screen at that moment, then answers or gets it done.

It doesn’t record your screen in the background. The answer appears next to the dot, and the chat is saved in Superagent for later.

Turn it on in Settings → General.

XII.Open & localオープン・ローカル

Don't trust. Verify.

An agent with a browser can use your logins. So every line of Superagent is public: read it, build it yourself, and check what it does.

Open source, all of it.

The Mac app, the iPhone app and the relay are on GitHub under the MIT license.

  • Read the code. See how it uses your browser, what leaves your Mac and how phone messages are encrypted.
  • Build it yourself. Clone it, change it, ship your own.
  • Signed and notarized by Apple. The app you download is the build that was published.
  • Issues in the open. Bugs and plans are discussed in public.

No server of ours.

Your work goes nowhere but your own disk, and the code shows it.

  • Stays on your Mac. Chats, code and pages are never uploaded.
  • Your own subscription. Claude Code talks to Anthropic, Codex to OpenAI, Antigravity to Google. Directly, with no second bill.
  • No account, no tracking. No sign-up and no analytics in the app.
  • Your logins stay yours. Browser sessions are stored on your Mac, like any browser’s.
  • Your phone, encrypted. Only when you pair a phone does the Mac use a relay, and it sees nothing but encrypted data. You can run your own with one command.

Read the code for the Mac app, the iPhone app and the relay, or see all three in one place.
