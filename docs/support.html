The iPhone app needs a Mac

The iPhone app is a companion to Superagent on your Mac: it shows your Mac's projects and agents and lets you answer them from anywhere. If you do not have a Mac set up yet, tap Try the demo on the first screen to look around with sample data.

Pairing a Mac

  1. Install Superagent on your Mac from superagent.computer.
  2. On the Mac open Settings → Phone and choose Show pairing code.
  3. In the iPhone app tap Pair a Mac and scan the code.

The Mac has to be awake and online for the phone to reach it. Superagent keeps it awake by default while a phone is paired.

Common questions

The phone says it cannot reach my Mac. Check the Mac is on and Superagent is open, then pull down to refresh or tap the Mac in Settings.

I did not get a notification. Allow notifications for Superagent in iOS Settings, and keep the phone paired.

How do I remove a Mac? Settings → Paired Macs, swipe the Mac left. On the Mac you can remove the phone in Settings → Phone.

What does it cost? Superagent is free. The coding agent you use with it has its own plan.
