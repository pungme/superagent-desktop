It never comes forward.

The one rule the whole app is built around. Agents finish in the background and send a notification with what they actually said. The window rises only when you ask it to — not when it is done, not when it has a question, not ever.

So you can leave it running.

Sessions start on your first message and wind down when they go idle. Panes, open files and the active chat survive a restart, and an update. Routines keep watching a page on a timer whether the window is in front of you or not.

A real browser

It navigates, clicks, types and reads back — in the browser on your screen, with the sessions you are already signed into.

An iPhone in the window

A real iOS Simulator streamed from its own framebuffer. It installs the build, launches it, and you can tap it yourself.

A board it keeps

Backlog, next, doing, done — per project, and Claude moves the cards as the work lands.

Worktree chats

A conversation on its own git branch and directory. It works; your checkout does not move.

Routines

“Check this page every hour,” in plain language, on a timer — and it tells you what changed.

Talk instead of typing

Hold ⌥Space and say it. Transcribed on your own Mac; your voice never leaves the machine.
