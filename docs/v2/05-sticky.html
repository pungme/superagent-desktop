01 — SIDE BY SIDE

Ask for a change, watch the page change.

The chat sits next to a real browser. No alt-tabbing to find out whether it worked: the page reloads in the same window, in front of you, while the conversation carries on.

02 — THE BROWSER

It drives the browser you are logged into.

Open a page, click, type, read it back. Not a hidden headless browser it describes to you second-hand — the one on your screen, with your sessions. Take the mouse back whenever you like.

03 — THE FILES

The tree, the diff and the chat, together.

Click any file to read it beside the conversation — source, markdown, images, PDFs. Every edit shows up the moment it happens, with just the change highlighted.

04 — EVERYTHING IN ITS PLACE

Projects the way you think about them.

Plain browser tabs on top, projects below, each conversation nested underneath — with a spinner while it works, a dot when it needs you, and the branch where you would expect it.

05 — AND IT LEAVES YOU ALONE

Short settings, quiet behaviour.

Including how much the agent may do without asking. It finishes in the background and sends a notification; the window comes forward only when you say so.
