SuperAgent
Version 1.2 · macOS
Free & open source

Your agent needs a computer.

Every coding agent on the market is a conversation in a terminal. It writes what it did, and you take the rest on trust. This one gets a screen — a real browser it drives, an iPhone it can tap, a desk it keeps its work on — and does it where you can see.

Runs on your machine, on your existing Claude subscription. Nothing is sent anywhere it wasn't already going. Signed and notarized by Apple; updates itself in the background.

§ 01

A real browser

Not a screenshot service. A Chromium view the agent drives — it reads the page structure, clicks by name, fills forms, and you watch every step of it happen.

§ 02

An iPhone in the window

A real iOS Simulator, streamed inside the app from its own framebuffer. Tap it, type into it, watch a build install. Nothing to grant, nothing to install.

§ 03

A desk, not a slot

The page and the phone sit side by side on one surface you arrange yourself, over a Monet you can switch off.

§ 04

A board it keeps

Backlog, next, doing, done — per project. Cards move themselves as the work happens, and each remembers the conversation that raised it.

§ 05

Worktree chats

Start a conversation on its own git worktree. The agent works an isolated branch; your checkout stays exactly as you left it.

§ 06

It stays out of the way

Agents finish quietly and send a notification with what they actually said. The window never shoves itself in front of what you were doing.

Three steps, then it is yours.

01
Install it
Download the DMG and drop it in Applications. If you already use Claude Code in the terminal, there is nothing else to set up.
02
Point it at something
A project folder, a URL, or a blank browser tab. Projects keep their own chats, board and panes.
03
Talk to it
Ask it to build, browse, test or explain — and watch the work happen in the pane next door.
